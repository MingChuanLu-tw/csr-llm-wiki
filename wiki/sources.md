## ⚠️ Two assurance levels — do not mix them on a slide

- **FULL** (source 1): equations re-derived symbolically (A4), two rounds of
  independent review against the page images (A3), all numbers reproduced.
- **light** (sources 2–5): read and summarised only. **No symbolic verification,
  no independent review.** Equations and numbers on those pages are transcribed,
  not checked.

## How sources 2–5 were chosen

Seven other papers were available locally. The four selected all model
**strategic CSR in a duopoly** — Kopel's own question — so the concept layer
could become genuinely cross-source rather than five disconnected summaries.
That is what produced [[rival-exclusion]] and [[csr-modelling-choices]].

Not selected, as further from the question: Shuai (2022) on upstream market
structure and partial ownership; a note on ownership and mergers; Wang (1998).
