## The spine, in one line

The leader keeps deterring the follower, keeps out-investing it — and still
loses on profit. That is sharper than "spillovers wreck CSR leadership", and it
is what the numbers actually support.

## Decisions taken in this outline

1. **Act V is the climax, not the conclusion** — the three-thresholds result lands at
   slide 22–23, leaving 7 slides to contextualise and open the discussion. Putting
   it last would end on our own calculation rather than on the room.
2. **Two honesty slides are in the main deck** (14, 24, plus 27). They are not
   backup. With an advisor present, the unproved lemmas and the limits of our own
   arithmetic are better raised by us than found by them.
3. **The derivation is compressed to slides 10–12.** The audience is graduate
   economists; full backward induction would eat 10 minutes and the propositions
   are in the backup if wanted.
4. **The four light sources get one slide (26), explicitly flagged as unverified.**
   They earn their place through [[rival-exclusion]] and [[csr-modelling-choices]],
   not through their own results.
5. **Figures are plotted from the verified expressions**, not copied from the PDF —
   the paper is CC BY 4.0 so copying would be permitted, but plotting lets us
   annotate and guarantees the curve matches what we checked.
